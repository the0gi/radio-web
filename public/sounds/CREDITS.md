Processing: excerpts from public HQ previews; converted to mono 24 kHz 16-bit PCM WAV; DC offset removed; four-second equal-power wrap crossfade; RMS and peak normalization. They are edited excerpts, not the original recordings. File provenance is in research/sound-sources.json and processing measurements in research/sound-loop-checks.json.

No sounds were taken from Apple or the reference app. No Moodist or Todak assets or source code were copied. Those catalogs were consulted for discovery only; the licensed audio previews came directly from Freesound.
